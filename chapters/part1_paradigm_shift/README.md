# Part I: The Paradigm Shift — From Prompts to Agentic Harnesses

Chapters 1 through 4 explore the fundamental breakdown of naive prompt engineering and establish the formal foundation of modern Cognitive Runtimes.

- **Chapter 1**: The Death of Prompt Engineering and the Rise of Cognitive Runtimes (`core/runtime/harness.py`)
- **Chapter 2**: Reasoning Models, Test-Time Compute, and Inference Scaling Laws (`core/intelligence/cognitive_router.py`)
- **Chapter 3**: The Architecture of the AI Harness: Scaffolding, Gateways, and Control Planes (`core/gateway/model_gateway.py`)
- **Chapter 4**: The Tool Execution Plane: Schemas, Structured Outputs, and Grammar Constraints (`core/tools/tool_registry.py`)
